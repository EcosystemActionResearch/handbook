# Getting Started with EAR Handbook

This guide is designed to help you navigate the handbook based on your goals. Whether you are a new member, launching a project, or contributing documentation, follow the path that matches your needs below.

---

## 🗺️ For the New Member (Onboarding)
If you are new to EAR or thinking about exploring an opportunity or research project for the first time, follow these steps to onboard to how we work.

1.  **Understand EAR:** Start by reading the [Introduction](index.md). This gives you an overview of our mission and scope.
2.  **Membership & Governance:** Review the core agreements:
    *   [Membership Agreement](../agreements/membership.md): Details how to join and what is expected.
    *   [Operating Model](../agreements/operating-model.md): Explains the structural framework of EAR.
3.  **Services Overview:** Familiarize yourself with the available support systems:
    *   [Internal Services Index](../agreements/internal-services/index.md): A comprehensive list of services (Care, Commons, Editorial, etc.).

## 🚀 For the Project Initiator (Idea to Reality)
If you have a new idea or opportunity and want to move it toward becoming an official initiative, follow this process flow.

1.  **Identify Opportunity:** Start by reviewing potential areas for growth in [Opportunities](../guides/research-projects/opportunities.md).
2.  **Define Scope & Principles:** Before proceeding, ground your idea in our established principles:
    *   [Research Principles](../agreements/research-imagination/research-principles.md): Understand the guiding philosophies.
    *   [Decision Templates](../agreements/decisions/decision-templates.md): Use these templates to structure how decisions are made.
3.  **Formalize & Execute:** Follow the steps outlined in [EAR research projects](../guides/research-projects/index.md) to move from concept to action.
4.  **Secure Support:** Determine if you need resources by checking [Request financial support](../guides/request-financial-support.md) or [Internal coordination](../guides/internal-coordination.md).

## ✍️ For the Contributor (Improving the Handbook)
If your goal is to improve, update, or add content to the handbook itself, follow these steps:

1.  **Read Guidelines:** Start with the official contribution guide: [How to contribute](../guides/contribute/index.md).
2.  **Learn the Tools:** Review the technical documentation on [MKDocs](../guides/contribute/mkdocs.md) for setup and usage.
3.  **Suggest Improvements:** If you find outdated information or a missing topic, use the suggestion form in the footer of any page to report it!

---
*This guide is intended as a starting point. Always refer to the specific agreement or guide for detailed rules.*
