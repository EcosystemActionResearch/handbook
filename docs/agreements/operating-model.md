# Operating model agreement

The EAR operating model is made up of five core functions.

1. [Research & innovation](research-imagination/index.md): 
    1. Action research programming in partnership with public interest internet ecosystem workers and institutions.
    2. Imagination programming in partnership with those affected, including artists, creators, and end-users.
2. [Communications](communications.md): Internal and external strategic communications to keep members and the broader EAR community informed.
3. [Operations and member services](internal-services/index.md): Inside and now enablemenet, meeting member needs and providing participation pathways and support.
4. [Steering](steering.md): Outside and future awareness, strategic development, and prioritization. All EAR members are welcome to participate in steering if so inclined.
5. [Project committee](project-committee.md): Policy and project governance, including accountability to the project's parent organization, the [Raft Foundation](https://raft.foundation).
